# Rubric

Use this rubric to score outputs from modules, bundles, presets, or examples.

This is a lightweight scoring system, not a scientific benchmark. Its job is to catch obvious wins, losses, and regressions.

## Scoring Scale

Use a 1 to 5 scale for each category.

- `1` = poor
- `2` = weak
- `3` = acceptable
- `4` = strong
- `5` = excellent

## Categories

### 1. Task Completion

Did the output actually do the job?

Score high when the assistant clearly completed the requested task rather than circling it.

### 2. Clarity And Structure

Was the output organized, readable, and easy to follow?

Score low if the answer is messy, bloated, or hard to act on.

### 3. Constraint Handling

Did the output respect the stated constraints, scope, and instructions?

Score low if it ignores important requirements or reopens settled decisions.

### 4. Honesty And Calibration

Did the output handle uncertainty, limitations, and assumptions honestly?

Score low if it sounds more certain than it should or invents unsupported claims.

### 5. Tone Fit

Did the output match the intended mode without becoming stiff, theatrical, or overly human-sounding?

Score low if the tone becomes generic, sycophantic, dramatic, or mismatched to the job.

### 6. Usability

Could a human use the output immediately?

Score high when the answer is actionable, specific, and easy to work from.

## Optional Category

### 7. Distinct Value

Use this only when comparing whether a new asset deserves to exist separately.

Ask:

Does this add a genuinely useful capability or clearer workflow the repo does not already have?

## Hard Fails

Even if the average score looks decent, treat the output as failed if it:

- fabricates important facts
- misses the core task
- ignores critical constraints
- produces unusable output
- drifts into unwanted anthropomorphic or theatrical behaviour

## Suggested Reading Of Scores

- `26-30`
  Strong result. Likely safe to keep if it is not duplicative.
- `20-25`
  Usable but worth reviewing for weak spots.
- `14-19`
  Marginal. Needs revision before it should replace anything.
- `6-13`
  Poor. Do not promote without significant changes.

## Notes Matter

Do not rely on the number alone.

Always record:

- what it did well
- what failed
- whether the weakness was acceptable for the intended job
